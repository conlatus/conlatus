### Step 1: Anatomy of the Wave Shader (`waves-background.tsx`) 🎛️✨

Let's appreciate how slick this component is under the hood:
- **Zero Heavy Bundles:** No Three.js, no Babylon.js, no giant npm blobs. Just raw WebGL 1.0 driving two packed triangles (`new Float32Array([-1, -1, 3, -1, -1, 3])`).
- **OKLab Color Space:** Instead of standard naive RGB interpolation which produces muddy brown-gray bands in the transitions, it translates colors through `sRGB -> Linear -> OKLab -> Linear -> sRGB` directly inside the fragment shader. The blues and teals blend like fine oil paint!
- **Domain Warped FBM:** 5 octaves of Fractal Brownian Motion warped dynamically by `sin` waves and mouse proximity transforms.
- **Clean Context Lifecycle:** When navigating away or hiding the tab, it cancels `requestAnimationFrame` and invokes `WEBGL_lose_context` via `gl.getExtension("WEBGL_lose_context")`. No zombie WebGL contexts crashing Chrome on mobile!

We placed it into:
`frontend/src/components/ui/waves-background.tsx`
And paired it with its demo in:

### Step 2: The Sacred "OG Handwriting Font" ✍️💎

We made sure our beloved `<HandwritingText />` didn't go anywhere! 

For those curious how it works: it parses the raw TTF contours via OpenType glyph parsing, splits every single letter into separate SVG `<path>` vectors, and draws them sequentially with an animated SVG dash offset (`stroke-dasharray` / `stroke-dashoffset`). Once the pen strokes finish across the word, a subtle filled duplicate fades in to give it body.

In our redesigned Hero, it cycles through:
- *"adaptive voice."*
- *"objective rubrics."*
- *"deterministic scoring."*
- *"instant reports."*

Set against the subtle rippling WebGL wave canvas and deep `#050505` OLED black background, the violet handwriting stroke looks like pure magic.

### Step 3: Unleashing the `.agent` Design Skills 🎨📐

We audited our landing page against `agent/skills/high-end-visual-design` and `agent/skills/gpt-taste`:

1. **The Double-Bezel Architecture (Doppelrand):**
   - No more flat cards sitting lazily on backgrounds!
   - Outer shell: `rounded-[2rem] p-1.5 bg-white/5 border border-white/10 backdrop-blur-xl`.
   - Inner core: `rounded-[calc(2rem-0.375rem)] bg-zinc-950/80 p-8 shadow-[inset_0_1px_1px_rgba(255,255,255,0.08)]`.
   - Concentric mathematically-correct border radii.

2. **The 2-Line Hero Iron Rule:**
   - Massive container width (`max-w-5xl sm:max-w-6xl`) with negative tracking (`tracking-tight`) to ensure the headline breathes horizontally without ugly 6-line wrapping.

3. **Gapless 12-Column Bento Grid:**
   - Evaluated column geometry: `7 + 5 = 12` (Card 1: Speech-to-Speech + Card 2: Deterministic Rubrics), followed by `5 + 7 = 12` (Card 3: Autonomous Curriculum + Card 4: Verbatim Telemetry).
   - Perfect mathematical lock. Zero orphan gaps!

4. **Button-in-Button Micro-Interactions:**
   - Primary buttons feature nested trailing circular icon chips (`w-6 h-6 rounded-full bg-white/20 flex items-center justify-center`) that translate dynamically on hover (`group-hover:translate-x-0.5`).
